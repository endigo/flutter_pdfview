## 1.0.0-beta.1

- Initial release: the AndroidPdfViewer / PDFium engine that `flutter_pdfview`
  1.x bundled, split out as an opt-in Android implementation for apps that need
  horizontal paging (`swipeHorizontal`, `pageFling`, `pageSnap`) or Android
  5.0–8.1. Behaviour is unchanged from `flutter_pdfview` 2.0.0-beta.1's Android
  engine.
